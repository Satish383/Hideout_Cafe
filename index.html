Our Story

Your Little Escape from the Everyday

Tucked away in the heart of Siwan, Hideout Cafe is a warm, welcoming space designed for slowing down and savouring the moment. Whether you're catching up with friends, enjoying a quiet coffee break, or treating yourself to something delicious — we've created a hideout just for you.

With rich aromas, cozy corners, and a menu crafted for comfort, every visit is an invitation to relax, unwind, and make yourself at home.
